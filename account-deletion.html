구매 기록은 관련 법령에 따라 결제 대행사에 보관되며, 당사 서버에서는 삭제됩니다.

처리 기간

앱 내 삭제는 즉시 처리되며, 서버 데이터는 30일 이내 완전히 삭제됩니다.

Account & Data Deletion

App: Warrior in Dungeon: Action RPG
Developer: NextEntry

Delete from within the app

Launch the game → Settings → Account → Delete Account → Confirm

If you have already uninstalled the app

Email nextentryco@gmail.com with your account information (the email used to sign in, or your recovery code). Requests are processed within 7 business days.

Data that is deleted

Account information, game progress (level, items, skills), and recovery code.

Data that is retained

Purchase records are retained by the payment provider as required by law. They are removed from our servers.

Processing time

In-app deletion takes effect immediately. Server-side data is fully deleted within 30 days.

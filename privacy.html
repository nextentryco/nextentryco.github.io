Privacy Policy

Warrior in Dungeon · NextEntry · Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how NextEntry ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Warrior in Dungeon (the "Game"), available on the App Store and Google Play.

The Game does not display advertising, does not use third-party analytics or tracking software, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information We Collect

The Game requires a network connection. We collect only what is needed to run your account and the game service:

  • Account identifier — an identifier provided by Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, or a randomly generated identifier if you play as a guest. We do not receive your name or email address through these sign-in methods.
  • Device identifier — a device or installation identifier, used to link a guest session to your saved data and to keep your progress on the correct device.
  • Game data — character level, experience, items, currency, stage progress, skills, settings, and save slots.
  • Purchase records — transaction identifiers returned by Apple or Google when you make an in-app purchase, used to verify the purchase and deliver the item. We never receive or store your card or payment details.
  • Connection information — IP address, app version, and connection timestamps, recorded in server logs for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or browsing activity. We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not use any identifier for advertising, marketing, or cross-app tracking.

How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account and save your progress
  • To verify in-app purchases and deliver purchased content
  • To restore an account using a recovery code
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the game service
  • To display in-game service notices

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Third Parties

The Game uses the following services. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store in-app purchases
  • Google — Google Sign-In, Google Play in-app purchases
  • Unity — game engine

We may disclose information if required by law, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, investigate fraud, or respond to a lawful government request.

Recovery Codes

You can issue a recovery code from the in-game recovery page, regardless of how you sign in, so you can move your progress to another device. Anyone who has your recovery code can transfer your game data to their account, so please keep it private. A recovery code can be used only once; a new code is issued afterward.

Deleting Your Account

In the Game: Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently removes your account identifier, game data, save slots, and recovery code from our servers. This cannot be undone, and deleted data cannot be restored.

If you have uninstalled the Game, you may request deletion by emailing your recovery code to nextentryco@gmail.com. See the account deletion page for details. Uninstalling the Game alone does not delete your account data from our servers.

After deletion you may sign in again with the same Apple or Google account; you will be treated as a new player and will start from the beginning.

Data Retention

We retain account and game data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the data is removed from our active database. Server logs containing connection information are retained for a limited period for security purposes and are then deleted.

Security

Game data is stored on access-controlled servers and transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at nextentryco@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a revised update date. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: nextentryco@gmail.com

게임을 이미 삭제한 경우, 복구 코드를 nextentryco@gmail.com으로 보내 삭제를 요청할 수 있습니다. 자세한 내용은 계정 삭제 안내 페이지를 확인하세요. 게임을 기기에서 삭제하는 것만으로는 서버의 계정 데이터가 삭제되지 않습니다.

삭제 후 같은 Apple 또는 Google 계정으로 다시 로그인할 수 있으며, 이 경우 신규 이용자로 처음부터 시작합니다.

About the game

Warrior in Dungeon is an action RPG for Android and iOS, made by NextEntry. Each stage throws a new wave of monsters at you. Clear it, collect items and currency, learn new skills, and push deeper.

Your progress is saved to your account, so you can pick up where you left off.

Signing in

You can play as a guest, or sign in with Google or Apple to keep your progress tied to your account. When you sign in with Google, the game receives only an account identifier. It does not receive your name or email address, and it does not access any other data in your Google Account.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account

In the game, go to Settings, then Account, then Delete Account. You can also request deletion on the account deletion page or by emailing nextentryco@gmail.com.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or account issues: nextentryco@gmail.com
